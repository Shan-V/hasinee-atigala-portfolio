About

I'm an urban and regional planner with experience at both the metropolitan planning organization level in the United States and the national authority level in Sri Lanka. I currently work with UN-Habitat on disaster-resilient resettlement and recovery in Nuwara Eliya District, within an integrated UN–Government program. My work centers on strategic planning, comprehensive plan-making, and stakeholder engagement, with exposure to regulatory frameworks at multiple scales.

I hold a Master of Urban & Regional Planning from Ball State University (3.9 GPA) and a B.Sc. (Hons) in Town & Country Planning from the University of Moratuwa. I'm a chartered Town Planner with the Institute of Town Planners Sri Lanka and a member of the American Planning Association.
